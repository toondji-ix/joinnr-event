## Synthesis and disposition

The external research gives a credible basis to investigate networking motivation, initiation friction, reciprocal learning and accessibility trade-offs. It does not establish that local attendees will complete onboarding or that Joinnr improves meaningful conversations. Research coverage is heavily scientific/medical/library and includes pandemic-era virtual settings; local conference categories, nonresponse and commercial-source bias remain explicit gaps.
